BODY ARCHITECTURE: Model M-026 "Pure Form"

INTERNAL FRAME:
Instead of massive external plates, Artho is equipped with an endoskeleton made of titanium-ceramic composite. This skeleton is 5 times stronger than a human's, yet possesses the flexibility that allows Artho to perform acrobatic maneuvers unavailable to his "armored" counterparts. The claws on his hands are a direct extension of this endoskeleton — they are not covered by artificial skin or fur, remaining completely exposed.

MODULARITY:
Despite the absence of fixed armor, Artho retains full limb modularity on par with standard armored protogen models.
- "Quick-Swap" connectors: Artho can detach his arms or legs for maintenance or replacement with specialized tools in just 30 seconds.
- Magnetic fasteners: Joints are held by powerful electromagnetic fields, minimizing friction and wear.

LIFE SUPPORT SYSTEM:
1. Nanites: Billions of microscopic robots circulate in his biological component. They act as an immune system, instantly "welding" soft tissue damage and cleaning visor filters.
2. Bio-reactor: Processes organic food into clean energy. Excess energy is released as gas, like all protogens.
3. Visor: The only external part, made of hardened polycarbonate mixed with nanites. It is not a helmet — it is part of his head, directly connected to the brain's neural network. It can open its mouth to consume food.

OVERLOAD MODE:
In moments of intense stress or processing large amounts of data, the nanites in his body begin to work at their limit, causing a faint emerald glow along the entire endoskeleton.

DIRECT INTERFACE:
Artho does not use a keyboard. When connecting to the network via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, he literally merges with the data stream. For instance, poor ping is experienced as a slight dizziness.

CONCLUSION: Vulnerability to physical attacks is compensated by incredible reaction speed and self-repair capabilities.

[ SYSTEM NOTICE ]
Emergency system override is disabled by default. To manually trigger a core reboot or access deep-layer diagnostics, the operator must input the unique authorization sequence via the terminal. Reference key: "Subject Name".
